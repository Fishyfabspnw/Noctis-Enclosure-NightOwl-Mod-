Blender 5.0.1 / Cycles, 96 samples with denoising, area-light softboxes, and a dark blue-gray reflective floor reproduce the earlier GitHub studio style. Product-directed lighting preserves the darker background. The USD export retains the visible assembly's placement and 3,341 body meshes.

No product geometry was generated or redesigned. Camera orientation, material roughness/color response, and transparent-panel shading were adjusted for readability. Presentation materials are not manufacturing specifications. The images do not establish fit or thermal performance. Hidden bodies from the complete STEP are not shown.

[Earlier repository images](../docs/LEGACY-IMAGES.md) are retained for project history.
